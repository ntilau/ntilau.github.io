Site note

What Zunga is — and what it isn’t.

Zunga is a static, illustrative hospitality prototype built to explore accommodation opportunities referenced in the Rwanda Formula 1 & Tourism Investment checklist. All listings, prices, ratings and reviews are sample data — they are not real, bookable properties.

This demo has no payment processing, no accounts and no backend. Bookings are stored temporarily in your browser only (localStorage) and are cleared when you start a new booking.

Official F1 branding, tickets and paddock access require separate authorization and are not part of this project. Actual demand and pricing must be verified before any investment decision.
